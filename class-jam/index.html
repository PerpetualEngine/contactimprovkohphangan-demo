More about the Focused Jams

Jam guidelines

To create a safe and fruitful dance space of mutuality.

Arrive and listen

  • Take time to settle physically and energetically into the space.
  • Listen deeply: to yourself, your partners, and the space.

Clothing and safety

  • Wear long pants and shirts for skin coverage to avoid friction discomfort.
  • Remove jewellery or items that might catch on others.
  • Bring an extra shirt as tropical weather brings sweaty dances and it's nice to refresh.
  • Contact Improvisation involves physical risk. Stay within your skills and comfort, and take care of your safety.
  • Keep the floor for dancing; observe or rest from the sides.

Boundaries and consent

  • Respect boundaries: it's okay to say or hear "no" without explanation.
  • Practice reading non-verbal cues; ask for clarity if unsure.
  • Be mindful of how your energy and actions affect others in the space.
  • The focus of the practice is not sexual or romantic. Please respect that in the space.

Support and communication

  • If uncomfortable, talk to your partner, the facilitator, or an experienced dancer.
  • Observing is a valid way to learn and contribute to the jam's energy.
