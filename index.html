Contact Improvisation Koh Phangan

Born from an unexpected pause in time, this island has become a sanctuary for dance and a living laboratory for contact improvisation.

Our focus is rooted in the physicality of contact improvisation as an evolving art form, inspired by the foundational teachings of Steve Paxton and the first generation of practitioners. We explore the body’s potential through the dynamics of touch, weight, and shared momentum, continually discovering how communication in contact informs both movement and life.
