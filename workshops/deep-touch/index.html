Deep Touch

3-Day Workshop with Rosalind Holgate-Smith

Register

On what it means to touch, and be touched deeply.

This workshop will introduce deep touch techniques including gradual weight loading, sticky touch, and hugging, holding, squeezing involving deep compression and release.

Each morning will begin with slow somatic bodywork and will focus on one of the techniques. We will dive through and awaken distinct layers of skin, fascia, fat, muscle and bone, working slowly to invite release and expanded spatial awareness so that we may progress into more dynamic dancing with enhanced receptivity, sensitivity and compassion.

Through the exploration of the deep touch techniques the workshop sets a framework of tools to investigate what it means to be touched deeply, physically, energetically and emotionally. The aim is to question how we can cultivate dances of greater depth, intimacy and expressive agency in Contact Improvisation?

Please come ready for somatic bodywork, flying, lifting, soft acrobatics and authentic interactions!

*Recommended for intermediate to advanced CI dancers.

About the teacher

Rosalind Holgate Smith (UK) is a Dance Artist and Somatic Educator who creates performances and installations that explore intimate connections between people, place and the environment. She specialises in teaching Contact Improvisation and Contemporary Release Techniques and takes inspiration from Body Mind CenteringⓇ, Authentic Movement, Skinner Release Technique, Martial Arts and her love for dancing outdoors. She has PhD on the Vocabulary of Touch and encountering Otherness in Contact Improvisation and is currently a Dance Lecturer for Middlesex and Minzu University, on Hainan Island in China. Besides working in academia Rosalind has also taught dance for diverse communities, including children, disabled adults and older people. She is additionally an experienced Yoga Instructor and following her passion for water, she teaches swimming with the Shaw Method, which integrates the Alexander technique.
