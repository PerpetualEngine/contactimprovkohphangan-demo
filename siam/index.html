Siam Home Of Moving Ideas

is created/curated with love by Alexandra Wuzyk and supported by the Towards Foundation.

It lives in the interest of clear transmission of contact improvisation, supporting artistic development and experimentation, and nurturing space and agency for individuals to develop themselves in their most honest and wholehearted capacity.
